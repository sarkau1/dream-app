# Flight Over the Sea

You lift off the horse's back into open air, the endless sea unrolling beneath you. Flying is the classic impossible-physics dream sign — and doing it on purpose, instead of stumbling into it, is most of the lesson.

## Choices

- **Fly toward the storm on the horizon** → [[The Eye of the Storm]]
- **Land on the water and walk on it** → [[Beneath the Waves]]
