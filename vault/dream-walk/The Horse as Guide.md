# The Horse as Guide

Instead of forcing anything, you ask the dream itself for help. The horse breaks into a gallop across water that now holds like glass underfoot. Ahead, a figure in a long coat stands perfectly still on the waves — someone, or something, the dream has made to meet you.

## Choices

- **Approach the figure** → [[The Waveborn Guide]]
- **Steer away and explore alone** → [[Flight Over the Sea]]
