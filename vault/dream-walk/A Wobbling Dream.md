# A Wobbling Dream

The stabilization fumbles. Colour drains from the edges of the scene and the horizon starts to pixelate, the way a dream does right before it collapses. This is recoverable — a decaying dream is a warning, not a verdict.

## Choices

- **Grab the reins hard, ground yourself in touch** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 4
  - success (≥4) → [[Stable Lucidity]]
  - failure (<4) → [[False Awakening (II)]]
- **Let it collapse, see where you land** → [[False Awakening (II)]]
