# The Eye of the Storm

The storm on the horizon is perfectly still at its centre. Standing in that stillness, riderless, is the horse — waiting exactly the way it was waiting at the very start. The dream is folding back on itself, and for the first time you can see the seam.

## Choices

- **Mount the horse again** → [[Awakening on the Tide]]
- **Refuse — hold your ground in the eye of the storm** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 5
  - success (≥5) → [[The Eye Holds]]
  - failure (<5) → [[Awakening on the Tide]]
