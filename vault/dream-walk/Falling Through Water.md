# Falling Through Water

The flight fails — you drop instead of rise — but the sea receives you gently and you keep sinking, slow as a held breath. In a lucid dream, failure is rarely a dead end. It's just a different door than the one you tried.

## Choices

- **Breathe underwater on purpose** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 4
  - success (≥4) → [[Beneath the Waves]]
  - failure (<4) → [[False Awakening (I)]]
- **Swim for the surface** → [[Flight Over the Sea]]
