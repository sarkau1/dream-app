# An Evasive Answer

*A dream character is present: **The Horse**.*

Whatever the horse said dissolves into wind before it finishes. Its mane flickers to smoke for half a heartbeat, then settles back to hair. Nothing was confirmed outright — but a seed is planted, and dream logic rewards a second question more than a first.

## Choices

- **Press it again** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 4
  - success (≥4) → [[First Reality Check]]
  - failure (<4) → [[Denial Drift]]
- **Let it go, follow the light on the water** → [[A Light on the Water]]
