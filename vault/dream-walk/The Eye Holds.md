# The Eye Holds

For one suspended moment, the loop doesn't reset. You stay in the eye of the storm, fully lucid, fully aware that this exact dreamscape has come around before — and that noticing the repeat is the real skill, more than anything you did with the horse or the sea. That recognition is what carries over when you wake for real.
