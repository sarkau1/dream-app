# First Reality Check

You look at your hands. The fingers ripple like reflections on water, and for a moment there are six of them, then five again. It clicks: this is a dream, and you are awake inside it. The realisation is the whole trick — reality checks work because you actually look, and actually ask.

## Choices

- **Stabilize the dream first — rub your palms, name what you see** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 4
  - success (≥4) → [[Stable Lucidity]]
  - failure (<4) → [[A Wobbling Dream]]
- **Don't wait — try to fly right now** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 5
  - success (≥5) → [[Flight Over the Sea]]
  - failure (<5) → [[Falling Through Water]]
- **Ask the horse to guide you** → [[The Horse as Guide]]
