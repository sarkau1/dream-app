# The Waveborn Guide

*A dream character is present: **The Waveborn**.*

Up close, the coated figure's face keeps almost resolving into someone you know, then sliding away from it. It offers you a small, plain question about the sea — the kind of test dream characters pose that ends up mirroring a doubt you already carry awake.

## Choices

- **Answer honestly, whatever comes to mind** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 3
  - success (≥3) → [[The Eye of the Storm]]
  - failure (<3) → [[An Evasive Answer]]
- **Refuse to play along, walk past** → [[Beneath the Waves]]
