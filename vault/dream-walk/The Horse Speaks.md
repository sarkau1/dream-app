# The Horse Speaks

*A dream character is present: **The Horse**.*

The horse turns its head further than a neck should turn and answers you in a voice that isn't quite a voice — more like a thought wearing sound. What it says is cryptic, half a riddle, half a memory that isn't yours. Dream characters rarely hand you the answer outright; they reflect back whatever you already half-know.

## Choices

- **Ask directly: "am I dreaming?"** — 🎲 roll a d6, DC 3
  - success (≥3) → [[First Reality Check]]
  - failure (<3) → [[An Evasive Answer]]
- **Ride on without pressing further** → [[Denial Drift]]
